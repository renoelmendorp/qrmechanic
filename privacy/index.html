Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

In short: QR Mechanic does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you create or scan stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. QR Mechanic has no servers and does not send the codes you create or scan, or anything else about you, to the developer or to third parties.

Your library

The codes you save, their folders and notes are stored on your device only. They are not synced to iCloud or uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your library.

Permissions

QR Mechanic asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it, and everything is processed on your device:

  • Camera, to scan QR codes. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Photos, to find codes in an image you pick and to save code images when you choose to.
  • Contacts and Calendar, to create a code from a contact or event you pick, and to add a scanned contact or event when you choose to.
  • Location, only when you tap to use your current location for a location code.

You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

Apple services

Searching for a place uses Apple Maps, and the Pro purchase is handled by the App Store. Apple processes that information under the Apple Privacy Policy. QR Mechanic only learns whether Pro is unlocked, never your payment details.

Links in scanned codes

When you open a link from a scanned code, it opens in your browser or the app it belongs to. Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies.

Children

QR Mechanic collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email qrmechanic.support@gmail.com.
