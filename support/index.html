How do I get Pro on a new iPhone?

Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account, open any Pro feature in QR Mechanic and tap Restore Purchase. If Family Sharing is on, family members get Pro the same way.

Why does my code say it may not scan?

QR Mechanic grades every styled code the way print-quality checks do. A low grade usually means there is too little contrast between the code and its background, for example a light gradient on white, or corner squares in a pale color. Make the code darker or the background lighter, or the other way around for light-on-dark codes, and the grade updates right away. Always test a printed code with a few phones before you hand it out.

Where are my saved codes?

Your library is stored on your iPhone only. It is not uploaded to us or synced to iCloud, and it is removed if you delete the app. To keep a copy of a code, export it as PNG, PDF or SVG, or save it to Photos.

Can I scan codes from a photo or screenshot?

Yes. In the Scan tab, pick an image from your photos or paste one, and QR Mechanic finds the codes in it. You can also share an image to QR Mechanic from another app.

Which sizes can I export?

PNG up to 4096 × 4096 pixels, with an optional transparent background. PDF and SVG are vector formats, so they stay sharp at any print size.
